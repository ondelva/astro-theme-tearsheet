Harbor Notes is a weekly letter on container freight: what a box costs to move, why the price changed, and what the schedule data said before the index did. It goes out every Tuesday at 07:00 Rotterdam time.

I'm Ines Varga. I spent nine years pricing Asia to Europe contracts for a mid-sized forwarder, which mostly meant reading carrier schedules until they started to look like weather. Harbor Notes is the analysis I used to write for our sales team, rewritten for anyone who buys, sells or forecasts freight.

## What you get

About two issues in five are free in full. The rest open with a free section and continue for members. Every issue carries a number and the week it covers, so the archive reads like a ledger: you can find what I said about the Suez detour in week 40 and check it against what happened.

Harbor Notes started in November 2025, took the first half of 2026 off while I changed jobs, and has gone out every Tuesday since July. New here? [Start here](/start-here).

## Corrections

When I get a number wrong, the correction runs as its own short issue so it shows up in the archive. The [corrections page](/corrections) lists every one.
