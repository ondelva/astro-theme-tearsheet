A newsletter about numbers gets some of them wrong. When I do, the correction runs as its own short issue, so it has a number, sits in the archive next to the issue it corrects, and reaches everyone who got the mistake by email. The original issue is not edited silently: it gains an **Updated** date and a line saying what changed.

Send corrections to [ines@example.com](mailto:ines@example.com). Say which issue and which number; a screenshot of the source helps.

## Corrections so far

- **Issue 104**, [A short note on a misprinted schedule](/issues/a-short-note-on-a-misprinted-schedule): demurrage free time at one Rotterdam terminal is 5 days, not 7.
- **Issue 111**, [The Suez detour is now the base case](/issues/the-suez-detour-is-now-the-base-case): updated on 1 October with a revised count of services through the canal.
