A free issue can carry one sponsor slot: three lines of plain text above the second heading, marked **Sponsor**. Members issues carry none. There are no banners, no tracking pixels and no sponsored issues.

| Slot                         | Readers |           Price |
| ---------------------------- | ------: | --------------: |
| One free issue               |   6,400 |   $650 an issue |
| Four free issues in a month  |   6,400 | $2,200 for four |
| Archive and Start here pages |   1,900 |    $900 a month |

Readers are subscribers on the day the issue goes out; the archive figure is monthly visitors. About half of subscribers work at a shipper or a forwarder, a quarter at a carrier or a terminal.

## What I don't take

- Carriers, alliances or terminals I write about. That is most of them.
- Anything that wants to sit next to a rate table.
- Copy I would not put my name under. I edit sponsor lines for length and plainness.

## Booking

Write to [ines@example.com](mailto:ines@example.com) with the dates you want. Slots are booked about six weeks ahead.
