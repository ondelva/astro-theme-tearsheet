## What is collected

When you subscribe, your email address is stored by the newsletter provider that sends the issues. Paid plans are billed by the provider's payment processor; this site never sees card details.

## Analytics

This site loads no analytics script by default. If the owner turns one on, page views are counted by that provider under its own terms.

## Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted, write to the address on the [About](/about) page.
